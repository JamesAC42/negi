# Turntable material assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the 3D Now Playing turntable. Original generated images are retained in Codex; these 1024px JPEG copies are the runtime assets.

- `turntable-walnut.jpg`: Seamless PBR albedo texture, flat evenly lit scan of beautiful dark warm walnut hardwood veneer. Fine dense horizontal grain with natural long flowing darker streaks and rich honey-brown narrow fibers. Uniform surface, no boards, seams, knots, perspective, lighting gradients, highlights, shadows, or text. Used on the thin front and sides of a precision hi-fi turntable plinth. Tasteful mid-dark brown, photoreal natural wood detail. Square texture only, fill every edge.
- `turntable-metal.jpg`: Seamless square PBR material texture, flat evenly lit albedo scan of premium black anodized aluminum used on a hi-fi turntable top plate. Extremely dark charcoal graphite, nearly black, subtle fine horizontal micro-brushing and faint natural satin surface grain. Uniform texture from edge to edge. No gradients, lighting highlights, shadows, borders, objects, scratches, seams, or text. Fine restrained detail, never coarse noise.

The visual target, intermediate screenshots, and browser QA scripts live in the ignored `.dream-loop/` workspace folder. Album artwork continues to come from the existing local artwork service.
